FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Here are some common questions about StudyHub NG.

Is StudyHub NG free?

StudyHub NG is being designed with free learning and practice resources. Additional premium features may be introduced in the future.

Which examinations does StudyHub NG support?

The platform is being developed primarily for examination preparation such as WAEC and JAMB.

Can I practise on my phone?

Yes. The website is being designed to work across phones, tablets and computers.

Can I see my previous scores?

Registered students can use the dashboard and results section to review their quiz performance.

Can teachers or schools work with StudyHub NG?

Partnership opportunities can be discussed through the contact form.

How can I report a problem?

Use the contact form and select Technical Support. Include as much information as possible about the problem.
